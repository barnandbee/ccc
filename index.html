How to play

January has come early, and it's draining the Seasonal Spirit out of Christmas. Run, jump and use your character's special move to get through four levels of January gloom, then face Blue Monday himself.

  • Move: ← → or A D. On a phone, slide a finger left or right.
  • Jump: ↑, W, Space or Z. On a phone, flick up. Hold the key, or keep your finger down, to jump higher.
  • Special move: X or Shift. On a phone, tap.
  • Prefer on-screen buttons? Pause the game and switch Controls to buttons.
  • Pause: P or Esc

Collect stars of Seasonal Spirit, find three hidden treasures in each level, and light the Christmas tree at the end. Jump on January's minions to cheer them up. If you run out of hearts, you'll go back to the last flag.

To get on the high-score board, do a full run: start at level 1 and finish all four levels in order. Restarting the level you're on is fine, but replaying an earlier level ends the run.

Each character is unlocked by a question about their Christmas card, so keep your card handy.
